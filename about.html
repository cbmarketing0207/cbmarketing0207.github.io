CBMarketing is an AI automation agency built specifically for service businesses that are tired of doing everything manually.

The Story

Built by someone who gets it

I'm Chris. I started CBMarketing because I kept seeing the same problem — great businesses losing leads, missing calls, and burning hours on follow-ups that could easily be automated.

The tools existed. AI was capable enough. But nobody was packaging it in a way that actually made sense for a dental office, a roofing company, or a med spa. So I built that bridge.

Every automation we build is custom — trained on your business, written in your voice, and designed to work quietly in the background while you focus on what you're actually good at.
