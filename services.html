AI Receptionist

Missed Call Text-Back

The second you miss a call, an AI sends a personalized SMS to that lead — keeping the conversation warm before they call your competitor. Most leads decide within 5 minutes of hanging up.

  • Instant automated response on missed calls
  • Custom message written in your brand voice
  • Includes booking link or call-back option
  • Works 24/7 including nights and weekends

Appointment Follow-Up Sequences

A multi-step automated sequence that follows up with every lead after a call or inquiry — via email or SMS — until they book, say no, or ask to be removed. No lead falls through the cracks.

  • 3–5 step sequence over 7–14 days
  • AI-personalized messages per lead
  • Email + SMS options
  • Auto-stops when they book

Appointment Reminders

Automated reminders sent 24 hours and 1 hour before each appointment. Reduce no-shows by up to 60% without your front desk having to make a single call.

  • Multi-step reminders (24hr + 1hr)
  • Confirm, reschedule, or cancel via SMS
  • Calendar sync included
  • Custom messaging per service type

Review Request Automation

After every completed job or appointment, your clients automatically receive a friendly request to leave a review on Google. More 5-star reviews, zero effort from you.

  • Timed to go out right after service completion
  • Direct link to your Google review page
  • Follows up once if no response
  • Custom message in your brand voice

Reactivation Campaigns

Your old client list is sitting on money. We build AI-written campaigns that reach back out to clients who haven't booked in 60, 90, or 180 days — with messages that actually get replies.

  • Segmented by last visit date
  • AI-personalized reactivation messages
  • Email + SMS options
  • Includes a special offer or incentive hook

Website Chat Agent

A trained AI chatbot embedded on your website that answers visitor questions, qualifies leads, and pushes them toward booking — all day, every day, without any staff involvement.

  • Trained on your services, pricing, and FAQs
  • Embeds on any website in minutes
  • Captures name, email, and inquiry details
  • Hands off to you when a lead is hot

Social Media Content

  • 30-day content calendar

  • Written in your brand voice
